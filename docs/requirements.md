---
title: Requirements
sidebar_position: 1.1
---

- PHP 8.4 or higher
- Laravel 12 or 13
- [javaabu/forms](https://github.com/Javaabu/forms) v2, [javaabu/stencil](https://github.com/Javaabu/stencil) v1, [javaabu/permissions](https://github.com/Javaabu/permissions) v3 and [javaabu/manifest](https://github.com/Javaabu/manifest) v1 (installed with the package)

Optional:

- [inertiajs/inertia-laravel](https://github.com/inertiajs/inertia-laravel) 2 or 3, with React 19, `@inertiajs/react`, `@javaabu/forms` and `@javaabu/onu`, for the Inertia React builder and the `<Menu>` component
- [javaabu/translatable](https://github.com/Javaabu/translatable) v3, to translate menu items ([Translations](advanced-usage/translations.md))
